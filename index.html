3D CONTROLS & MOVE GUIDE

KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  • A / D or ← / → - Move left / right
  • W or ↑ - Jump
  • S or ↓ - Crouch
  • F - Punch
  • H - Kick
  • G - Heavy slam
  • P - Special power
  • Q - Hold to guard

PHONE & TABLET

  • Drag the MOVE pad left or right to approach or retreat.
  • Tap PUNCH or KICK for your basic attacks.
  • Tap POWER for your fighter's special move.
  • Hold GUARD to block incoming attacks.
  • DAO SLAM launches a heavy close-range attack.
